Day 62 — REST DAY

Thursday, January 28, 2027 Rest

😴 Today is programmed rest

Full rest, no session

Ketan's principle for today: Absolute rest after ages — enjoy it; intensity returns soon

  • Sleep is the training input today — protect a full night.
  • Eat normally; keep your standing milestone (sugary drinks weekends + one weekday, one small snack).
  • Light walking or easy movement is fine if you feel like it — nothing that makes you sore.
  • Log your soreness (0–10) and sleep below so tomorrow's pre-check is honest.

🛑 Recovery & pain rules for rest days

Green: general muscle soreness that is fading day by day — normal, rest as planned.

Amber: a muscle still at 4–6/10 soreness tomorrow morning — tomorrow's session for that muscle drops ~10% load and one set per exercise.

Needs attention: soreness at 7+/10, soreness lasting past 72 hours, sharp or joint pain, numbness or tingling, or pain that changes how you move. Take an extra rest day, swap the next session for that muscle, and if it persists or keeps returning, get it checked by a physio/clinician rather than pushing through.

Your standing swap rule: if a muscle group is still very sore when its day comes up, swap that day with the next cardio or rest day. Keep the overall order.
