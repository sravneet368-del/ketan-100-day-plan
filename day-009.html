Day 9 — FIRST BACK & BICEP SESSION

Thursday, October 29, 2026 Pull — back & biceps

🎯 Today's session — Pull — back & biceps

Ketan doesn't state sets/reps for this one. Your default: 3 sets of 10–12 slow, controlled reps, 60–90 sec rest (2–3 min on heavy compounds), effort ~6–7/10. Aim to finish in about 60 minutes.

His principle: Master basic movements; technique beats weight lifted

✅ Before you start (60 seconds)

  • Soreness check for today's muscle group (0–10): 0–3 train as planned · 4–6 cut loads ~10% and drop one set per exercise · 7+ or sore past 72 hours — swap today with the next cardio/rest day instead.
  • Slept under 6 hours? Cap the whole session at 7/10 effort and skip any pyramid top sets.
  • Safety setup: squat and bench inside a rack, safety pins just below the bottom of the bar's path — every time. No spotter and unsure of a bench weight? Use the machine or dumbbell version today.
  • Never start a rep you aren't confident you can finish.

🔥 Warm-up (his rules — max 5 minutes)

  • Joint mobility, not static stretching: shoulder rotations (PVC pipe or band), cat-camel, easy bodyweight version of today's first movement.
  • Then today's first exercise with a light weight for 1–2 easy sets, well short of your working load.
  • Stretch after the workout, never before — 5 minutes on the muscles you trained ends the session.

🛑 Your stop rules for today (no-failure metrics)

You never train to failure in this plan. A set ends on the first of these triggers — not when your body gives out:

  1. Reps in reserve (RIR): barbell compounds stop with 3 reps left — always. Machines & cables: 2 left. Isolation (curls, raises, extensions, flyes): 2 left. Bodyweight: 3 left.
  2. Rep speed: the way up takes ~1 second. If it becomes a grind past a slow count of 3, that was your last rep. Re-rack; do not attempt the next one.
  3. Form break: the first rep with a breakdown (back rounding, knees caving, butt off the bench, swinging a row, hard back-arch on presses) is your last rep of the set.
  4. Range of motion: two reps in a row shorter/shallower than your early reps = set over. A partial rep is a stop signal, not a rep to chase.

After a speed, form or ROM trigger, the next set is 5–10% lighter (one plate less on a machine). That's automatic.

Green: muscle burn and effort — normal, continue.

Amber: joint/tendon ache 1–3/10 that stays the same — finish the set, add no load, swap the exercise if it returns next set.

Red — stop that exercise for the day: sharp/stabbing pain, joint pain over 3/10, numbness or tingling, or pain that changes how you move.

📈 Progression rule

Add weight next time only if all four happened today: every set hit the top of its rep range · last set still had 2+ reps in reserve · zero form triggers · zero amber/red pain. Then add the smallest step: ~+2.5 kg upper body, +5 kg lower body, one plate on machines. An exercise that misses twice in a row goes 10% lighter, not heavier.
